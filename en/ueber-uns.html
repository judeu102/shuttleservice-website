About AYDEN Chauffeurs & Limousines

Premium chauffeur service from Munich – for companies that rely on reliability and discretion.

Who We Are

Chauffeur & Limousine Service at Premium Level

AYDEN Chauffeurs & Limousines stands for first-class mobility in Munich and across Europe. We are the chauffeur service that corporate clients, PAs and office managers rely on – whether an airport transfer, a limousine service for business appointments or coordinated event shuttles for hundreds of guests.

What Sets Us Apart

Service That Makes the Difference

Uncompromising Quality

Our chauffeurs bring international experience, complete regular defensive-driving training and always appear in professional business attire. Every ride is monitored from planning to arrival – for maximum transparency and absolute reliability.

Premium Fleet

From the Mercedes S-Class and Audi A8 to the V-Class for groups: our fleet meets the highest standards. Every vehicle is impeccably maintained, fitted with premium amenities and ready to take you to your destination in style.

Connected Across Europe – Rooted in Munich

Our network spans the whole of Europe, while our roots are in Munich. Whether an airport transfer at MUC, a chauffeur service for a trade fair in Dusseldorf or a limousine service in Berlin – we know the local requirements and deliver reliably.

Flexibility for Event Planners & PAs

We work closely with you to integrate transport seamlessly into your event. From custom vehicle branding and branded reception counters to providing hostesses – we adapt to your requirements, not the other way around.

The Founder

Daniel Hankofer

Founder & Managing Director, AYDEN Chauffeurs & Limousines

AYDEN Chauffeurs & Limousines was founded by Daniel Hankofer – with the vision of offering business clients and travel planners across Europe a chauffeur service of the highest calibre. His many years of experience in the international chauffeur and limousine business form the foundation of the company.

Professionalism, discretion and consistent service quality are not buzzwords to him but the benchmark for every single ride. From the careful selection of experienced chauffeurs to the flawless organisation of complex transfers and events – Daniel Hankofer shapes every detail personally.

Under his leadership, AYDEN has grown into a trusted premium provider operating well beyond Munich – and one that corporate clients, PAs and office managers across Europe rely on.
