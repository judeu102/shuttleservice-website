Shuttle with Security Driver

More Than an Attentive Chauffeur

Some journeys call for more than an attentive chauffeur. For sensitive assignments, exposed individuals or situations with heightened security needs, we provide trained security drivers who keep an eye on both the vehicle and the passenger.

A security driver differs from a conventional chauffeur through additional training in defensive and evasive driving techniques, forward-looking route planning with security in mind, and the ability to recognise and respond quickly to dangerous situations. This makes them not just a driver, but an added layer of security throughout the entire journey.
