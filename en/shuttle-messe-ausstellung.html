Logistics That Adapt to Your Fair Schedule

Trade fairs come with tight time windows. The stand has to be staffed by opening time, key clients are expected at the airport, and there is often a get-together in the evening. We take care of the logistics between hotel, exhibition grounds and airport, so your team can focus on the fair.
