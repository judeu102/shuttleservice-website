Berlin

A capital between politics and the start-up scene

Berlin brings together politics, international congresses and a growing start-up scene in a compact space. Embassies, ministries and congress centres are often only a few minutes apart, which makes last-minute rescheduling a frequent occurrence.

What sets our chauffeur service in Berlin apart

Airport

BER Airport

The airport lies to the south-east of the city; the journey to the centre takes around 30 to 40 minutes. We monitor arrival times and adjust to any delays.

Politics

Government district

The Bundestag, the Federal Chancellery and numerous ministries are located in the centre. For appointments here, discretion and punctuality are especially important, and both are standard for our chauffeurs.

Congresses

CityCube and ICC

Both venues host major trade fairs such as ITB or IFA. We handle the transfer between hotels and the congress centre for entire delegations.

Hotels

The city’s addresses

Adlon Kempinski and Regent Berlin are among the establishments where we frequently collect international guests.
